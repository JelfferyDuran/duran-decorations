# Brief — Balloon arches & garlands

> Read `assets/brand/brand.json` first. Match `imagery.style`, respect `imagery.avoid`.

## Niche

Organic balloon arches and garlands — the signature Duran Decorations product.

## Objective

Build a royalty-free inspiration gallery of arch styles (not client work).
Generated concepts are NEVER presented as completed Duran Decorations client work.

## Style anchors

- Palette: two lanes — (a) premium: gold/champagne + deep plum/emerald; (b) playful: candy pink, tangerine, lime, sky.
- Light: warm and soft, balloons glossy, no harsh flash.
- Composition: arch fills 70%+ of frame; slight low angle for grandeur; leave clean negative space at top for headlines on social crops.
- Detail: varied balloon sizes, a few clear/confetti balloons, one accent element (neon word, tassel, floral sprig).

## Shot list

1. Full arch, straight-on, event-room context.
2. Close detail of balloon cluster texture.
3. Arch with neon sign accent ("oh baby", "let's party", birthday name placeholder — spelled correctly).
4. Outdoor/garden arch variant.

## Must avoid

- Deflated or wrinkled balloons; visible strings/tape; gray studio voids.
- Text that is misspelled or gibberish.
