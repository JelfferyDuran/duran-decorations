# Brief — Neon sign booths & backdrops

> Read `assets/brand/brand.json` first. Match `imagery.style`, respect `imagery.avoid`.

## Niche

Neon signs mounted on balloon walls, shimmer walls, and greenery backdrops —
the photo-booth moment.

## Objective

Royalty-free inspiration gallery (not client work). Generated concepts are
NEVER presented as completed Duran Decorations client work.

## Style anchors

- Palette: neon glow (pink/cyan/gold script) against deep plum or dark greenery.
- Light: dim ambient so the neon reads; glow bloom on surrounding balloons.
- Composition: sign centered, wall fills frame edge to edge; portrait 4:5 crops for Instagram.
- Text: short phrases only ("Glow Party", "Oh Baby", "Feliz Cumpleaños"); spelling must be perfect.

## Shot list

1. Neon script on full balloon wall, straight-on.
2. Neon on shimmer/sequin wall variant.
3. Close-up of the sign with bokeh balloons behind.
4. Wide booth scene with floor props (suggested, not cluttered).

## Must avoid

- Illegible or misspelled neon text; blown-out glow that washes out color.
- Empty dark rooms with no decor context.
