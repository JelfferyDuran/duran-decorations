# Brief — Dessert & candy tables

> Read `assets/brand/brand.json` first. Match `imagery.style`, respect `imagery.avoid`.

## Niche

Styled dessert tables, candy buffets, and cake displays with balloon framing.

## Objective

Royalty-free inspiration gallery (not client work). Generated concepts are
NEVER presented as completed Duran Decorations client work.

## Style anchors

- Palette: table linen in cream/blush; desserts in brand brights; gold risers and trays.
- Light: bright, appetizing, soft shadows. Food must look real and edible.
- Composition: table at slight three-quarter angle; balloon garland framing the top or sides; hero cake centered.
- Detail: varied heights (risers), one floral or balloon accent, no cluttered backgrounds.

## Shot list

1. Full styled table, three-quarter view.
2. Hero cake close-up with balloon bokeh behind.
3. Candy jars detail with gold scoops.
4. Kids-birthday variant (playful palette, character-free to avoid IP issues).

## Must avoid

- Melted/sloppy food; unlicensed characters or trademarked designs; cluttered backgrounds.
- Anything that could read as a real Duran Decorations client event.
