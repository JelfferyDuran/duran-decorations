Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?

As soon as you have your date! Popular weekends book up fast — 2–3 weeks ahead is ideal, and we'll always try to fit in last-minute requests when we can.

Why is your price lower than market?

We keep our overhead lean and pass the savings to you — typically $75+ below what similar decorators charge, with the same quality materials and craftsmanship.

What areas do you serve?

We're based in New Jersey and serve events across the state. Message us with your location and we'll confirm availability.

Do you do custom themes?

Yes! Custom themes are our favorite. Send us your inspiration — characters, colors, photos — and we'll design something one-of-a-kind.

Do you set up and take down?

Absolutely. We handle full setup before your event and clean take-down after — all you have to do is show up and celebrate.
